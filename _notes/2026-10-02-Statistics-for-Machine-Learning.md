These notes cover the statistical methods that drive data analysis and model evaluation in machine learning:

- **Descriptive Statistics**: Mean, median, mode, standard deviation, percentiles, and data summarization.
- **Hypothesis Testing**: Null and alternative hypotheses, p-values, significance levels, Type I and Type II errors.
- **Confidence Intervals**: Interval estimation, margin of error, and interpreting uncertainty in predictions.
- **Regression & MLE**: Ordinary least squares, maximum likelihood estimation, bias-variance tradeoff, and model selection.
