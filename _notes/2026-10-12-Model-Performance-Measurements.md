These notes cover how to measure and improve deep learning model performance:

- **Loss Functions**: Cross-entropy, MSE, hinge loss — choosing the right objective for your task.
- **Classification Metrics**: Accuracy, precision, recall, F1 score, and when each matters most.
- **Visualization Tools**: Confusion matrices, ROC-AUC curves, precision-recall plots, and learning curves.
- **Regularization**: Dropout, batch normalization, early stopping, and their effect on generalization.
