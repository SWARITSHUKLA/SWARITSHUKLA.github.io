These notes cover the probability foundations essential to understanding machine learning models:

- **Random Variables & Events**: Sample spaces, probability axioms, conditional probability, and independence.
- **Bayes' Theorem**: Prior, likelihood, posterior — the engine behind Bayesian inference and Naive Bayes classifiers.
- **Distributions**: Bernoulli, Binomial, Gaussian, Poisson — properties, PDFs, CDFs, and when each arises in ML.
- **Expectation & Variance**: Moments, law of large numbers, and their role in loss functions and estimators.
