These notes provide a handwritten, visual walkthrough of core linear algebra concepts and their direct applications in machine learning:

- **Vectors & Matrices**: Fundamental notation, matrix operations, transpose, dot products, determinants, and matrix inverses.
- **Linear Systems & Elimination**: Gaussian elimination, row operations, and Row Echelon Form (REF).
- **Vector Spaces & Distance Metrics**: Vector distances, $L_1$ and $L_2$ norms, orthogonality, and geometric projections.
- **Machine Learning Applications**: Best fit lines, linear regression formulations ($y = Wx + b$), weights and bias intuitions, single-layer perceptron representations, and dimensionality reduction.
