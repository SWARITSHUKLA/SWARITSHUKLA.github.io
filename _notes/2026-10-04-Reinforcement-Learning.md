These notes cover the fundamentals of reinforcement learning — agents learning through interaction:

- **Markov Decision Processes**: States, actions, rewards, transitions, discount factors, and the Bellman equation.
- **Value-Based Methods**: Q-learning, SARSA, value iteration, and temporal difference learning.
- **Policy-Based Methods**: Policy gradients, REINFORCE, actor-critic architectures, and advantage functions.
- **Exploration vs Exploitation**: Epsilon-greedy, UCB, Thompson sampling, and the exploration-exploitation tradeoff.
