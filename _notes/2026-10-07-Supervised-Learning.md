These notes cover the core supervised learning paradigm where models learn from labeled data:

- **Regression**: Linear regression, polynomial regression, cost functions, and gradient-based optimization.
- **Classification**: Logistic regression, decision boundaries, softmax, and multi-class classification.
- **Model Evaluation**: Train/test splits, cross-validation, precision, recall, F1 score, and confusion matrices.
- **Regularization**: L1/L2 penalties, overfitting vs underfitting, and the bias-variance tradeoff.
